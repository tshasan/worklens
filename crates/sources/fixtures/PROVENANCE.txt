Inactivity policy audit
  https://github.com/mozilla/bugbot/blob/master/bugbot/rules/inactive_reviewer.py
  https://github.com/mozilla/bugbot/blob/master/bugbot/rules/not_landed.py
  The former evaluates reviewer activity and old patches; the latter evaluates
  accepted, unlanded patches using a configurable number of weeks. Neither
  establishes a two-week policy for Jira in-progress tickets or arbitrary open
  revisions. Those branch-only stale cases were withdrawn. Existing review
  follow-up thresholds were preserved; text names latest-diff or modification
  age, rather than claiming that no activity occurred.
